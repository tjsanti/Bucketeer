# Overspending carries forward

A bucket's Available rolls into the next month whether it is positive or negative: Available = carried in + assigned − spent. An overspent bucket stays overspent until money is assigned to it. We rejected the YNAB-style alternative, which resets a negative bucket to zero each month and charges Ready to assign, because it hides the overspending. Covering an overspent bucket is always the owner's job and never happens automatically, not even in rain (see ADR-0005).
